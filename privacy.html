Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for DesertGecko and ReptileRecord

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Overview

DesertGecko and ReptileRecord respect your privacy. This privacy policy explains how the apps and support website handle information.

Covered apps

This privacy policy applies to the following apps:

  • DesertGecko — does not collect personal information.
  • ReptileRecord — stores animal record data locally on your device and, if you connect Firebase, syncs selected animal record data to Firebase for backup and use across your devices.

Personal information in the apps

DesertGecko does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information through the app.

ReptileRecord does not collect, sell, or share personal information for advertising or tracking. If you choose to connect ReptileRecord to Firebase, the app uses your signed-in account only to keep your own records separate from other users' records.

ReptileRecord Firebase storage

Firebase connection is optional. If Firebase is not connected, ReptileRecord records are stored locally on your device only.

If you connect ReptileRecord to Firebase, the following app data may be stored in Firebase:

  • Animal records you create in the app.
  • Measurements, length, weight, notes, and dates relevant to those animal records.
  • Photos that you add to animal records.
  • Licence records, including licence numbers, licence type or description, issue dates, expiry dates, reminder settings, and related notes.

No other personally identifiable information is intentionally stored by ReptileRecord in Firebase. ReptileRecord does not store passwords, payment information, advertising identifiers, or location tracking data in Firebase.

Firebase security

Firebase is provided by Google. Firebase services encrypt data in transit using HTTPS, and several Firebase services, including Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication, also encrypt data at rest. ReptileRecord uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

ReptileRecord stores Firebase data under the signed-in user's account so each user can access only their own records.

Analytics and tracking

DesertGecko and ReptileRecord do not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Data storage

Any app data you enter is stored locally on your device unless you choose to connect Firebase in ReptileRecord. When Firebase is connected, selected ReptileRecord animal, measurement, photo, and licence data is synced to Firebase for backup and use across your devices.

Support requests

If you contact support through the support form, the information you provide, such as your name, email address, device details, and message, will be used only to respond to your request.

The support form is processed by FormSubmit so that your message can be delivered by email.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please use the support form.
