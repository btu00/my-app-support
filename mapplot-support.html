What MapPlot does

  • Plan, view, and measure locations on marine maps.
  • Import and export waypoints, routes, and trails.
  • Discover and transfer data to compatible Lowrance sounders.
  • Remote-control and mirror a compatible sounder screen over the local network.

Before contacting support

  • Make sure the Mac and sounder are connected to the same local network.
  • Check that the sounder is powered on and its remote-control feature is enabled.
  • For import or export problems, include the sounder model and the exact message shown on screen.
  • For mirroring problems, include whether the video was stuck starting, disconnected, or showed an error.

Send a support request

Use the support form and include your macOS version, MapPlot version, sounder model, and steps to reproduce the problem.
